Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026 · Italiano · English · Español

In two lines

Amunì helps you organise group trips. It collects only the data needed to make that work, does not sell it, does not use it for advertising and contains no tracking or profiling tools of any kind. You can delete your account, with everything in it, directly from the app.

Who processes your data

The data controller is Andrea Sarzana, reachable at andreasarzana.lav@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

DataWhy it is needed
Email address, name and (if you set one) profile photo To create your account and let the other trip members recognise you. If you sign in with Google, we receive this information from Google and nothing more.
Date of birth and country of residence — both optional, asked once at first launch and skippable Your country of residence is used to give you information that is right for you: whether you need a plug adapter, which foreign ministry to consult, whether the European Health Insurance Card is valid at your destination. Your date of birth currently feeds no feature of the app: we store it for possible future use and you can decline to provide it without losing anything. Neither is visible to the other trip members: they are stored separately from your public profile and readable only by your own account.
Trip content: destinations, dates, places, accommodation, transport, notes, checklists, polls This is what the app is for. It is visible to the other members of the trips you take part in, and to no one else.
Expenses, shares and repayments To work out who owes what to whom. The app does not handle money: it does not process payments and does not connect to your accounts.
Payment references you enter yourself (PayPal, Revolut, IBAN) Only to show them to the other trip members when they need to pay you back. They are optional: without them the app works just the same.
Photos you upload to the trip board To share them with the group. They are kept in a private archive: only the members of that trip can see them, through temporary links.
Push notification identifier To let you know when something happens in the trip. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your phone settings.
Documents you upload yourself (images or PDFs: passport, ID card, insurance, tickets, vouchers…) So you have them with you while travelling. They are optional: the app works without them. Only you upload them and they are private by default: they are kept in a non-public archive and no one else can open them, not even the person who created the trip. A trip document becomes visible to the other members only if you turn sharing on, and you can turn it off at any time. Documents in your personal section (passport and the like) can never be shared. A copy stays on your phone, so you can open them without a connection; it is removed when you delete the document or the app. If you enter an expiry date, we use it only to warn you in time.

⚠️ We do not read the content of your documents and do not extract data from them automatically. An identity document is particularly sensitive data: upload it only if you really need to, and share it only with those who need to see it.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your device location. The app never asks for GPS permission: when you search for a place, only the text you typed is sent.
  • There are no ads, no profiling, and no analytics or tracking library of any kind inside the app.
  • We do not read your contacts, your messages or the other apps on your phone.
  • We do not sell or pass your data to anyone.

On what legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (art. 6.1.b GDPR) for everything needed to run the service: your account, trip content, expense calculations.
  • Consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR) for push notifications, which you grant to the operating system and can withdraw at any time.

Who it is shared with

Only with the technical providers needed to run the app, which process it on our behalf and not for their own purposes:

ProviderWhat it is for
SupabaseDatabase, authentication, and storage of photos and documents
GoogleSign in with Google and push notification delivery (Firebase)
ExpoPush notification delivery service
Mapbox and Photon (komoot)Search for the places you type; they receive the text you searched
Open-MeteoWeather forecasts for your destination
Viator (Tripadvisor group)Suggestions of bookable activities at your destination: it receives the trip destination name, never your identity. Any booking happens on Viator’s own site, under its own terms

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, under the safeguards provided by the GDPR (standard contractual clauses).

For how long

  • Board photos: they are deleted automatically three days after the trip ends. Trips without an end date have a limit of 50 photos instead.
  • Documents: they stay until you delete them, or until you delete your account. They have no automatic expiry: they are needed exactly when you need them, and deleting them by surprise would be worse than keeping them.
  • Everything else: as long as your account exists. When you delete it, your data is deleted.

How to delete your account

From the app: Profile → Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion removes your profile, your content and your photos. If you own trips with other participants, the app tells you first: you will need to delete or leave them, so the trip does not vanish for everyone else. Alternatively, write to the address above and we will take care of it.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its processing, object to it, or receive a copy in a readable format. Write to andreasarzana.lav@gmail.com. If you believe your data is being processed unfairly, you can contact the Italian supervisory authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the data protection authority of your own country.

Minors

The app is not intended for children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If the way we handle data changes, we update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes we also say so inside the app.

This website

The pages you are reading use no cookies, contain no scripts and load no resources from third-party servers: no fonts, no images, no external statistics. The only data the hosting service records is the technical minimum needed to serve the page.
